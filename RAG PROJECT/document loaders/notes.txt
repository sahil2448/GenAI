RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Notes

1. What is RAG?
RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
It is a technique used in AI systems where the model first retrieves useful information from an external source and then uses that information to generate an answer.
2. Why RAG is used
Large language models do not always know the latest information.
They can also give wrong or made-up answers.
RAG helps solve this by connecting the model to trusted documents, files, databases, or knowledge bases.
